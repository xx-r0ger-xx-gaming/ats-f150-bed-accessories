F-150 Camper Shell
Free community mod by RTech Digital (xx-r0ger-xx).

Adds a bed camper shell accessory for the Ford F-150 from the Road Trip: Ford DLC, with the shell
painted to match the truck body color. Requires American Truck Simulator and the Road Trip: Ford DLC.

All geometry and textures are original work. Not affiliated with or endorsed by SCS Software or
Ford Motor Company. Early development build: test in a separate profile first.
